The record contract

Rules the app keeps, so you don't have to.

A record book is only useful if you can trust it. These are built into Venpadi, not left to habit.

Nothing deletes.

A wrong sale, expense, debt or payment is reversed with a reason. It keeps its place in the list with a mark, so the full story is always there.

₦

Exact to the kobo.

Money is stored in kobo as whole numbers. ₦1,500.00 is always ₦1,500.00, with no rounding anywhere.

Totals are worked out, never guessed.

Stock on hand is the sum of every movement. A customer's balance is debts minus repayments.

Inventory is optional.

Tracking starts the first time you count an item. Until then it stays uncounted, never shown as zero.

It keeps records, not money.

Venpadi is not a wallet and not a bank. Nothing moves through it. Your PIN and fingerprint protect what you've written down.

FAQ

Straight answers.

Does Venpadi move money?

No. Venpadi keeps records of what you sold, spent, are owed and have in stock. It is not a wallet, a bank or a payment app, and nothing passes through it.

Does it work without network?

Yes. Every record saves on your phone first, whatever the network is doing. When there is network, it syncs to your account. Records waiting to sync only ever show a warning; nothing locks.

What if I record something by mistake?

You reverse it with a reason. The record stays in the list with a reversed mark, so your history stays complete and your totals stay honest.

Will my customers get SMS or WhatsApp reminders?

No. Reminders about overdue debts appear inside the app, for you. Venpadi never messages your customers.

Is Business Health a credit score?

No. It measures how complete your record keeping is, across four habits with fixed weights. It is recomputed every night and a fresh account has no score at all until the first sale.

Is there an online shop?

Not yet. An online shop is in the Venpadi design and is the next thing being built, but it is not in the app today. Like everything else in Venpadi, it will keep records only and will not take payments.

What if I lose my phone?

Log in on another phone with your number and PIN. Everything that reached your account syncs back. The first login on a new phone needs network.
